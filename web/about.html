About & roadmap

Built for the buses Punjab actually rides.

Big metros have real-time apps. Tier-2 towns and intercity highways mostly have a timetable and a guess. TransitPulse starts on one 21-km stretch of NH-44. The model is trained on synthetic GPS (notebook 01, seed 42). Its scores are not real-world accuracy.

SIH25013

Smart India Hackathon 2025 · Government of Punjab · Transportation & Logistics

Real-Time Public Transport Tracking for Small Cities

The problem statement asks for live bus tracking, arrival estimates and route information that work in small cities and on low-bandwidth connections. TransitPulse answers the arrival question with an honest window instead of a single minute.

Why tier-2 and intercity

Long headways make every minute expensive.

When the next bus is 30 minutes away, missing this one is costly, and waiting too early is too. A window plus a leave-by time is useful on a highway in a way a city-style countdown is not.

Corridor

Jalandhar → Phagwara

6 stops, 5 segments, 21.2 km, 35.5 min scheduled. Mixed city and highway traffic.

Rider

Students, workers, day trips

Stops include a university gate and two bus stands, where a missed bus costs real time.

Constraint

Patchy data, low bandwidth

Quality gates before the model; a light rider view that needs no map to be useful.

Roadmap

From one corridor to state fleets.

A plan, not a promise. Every later phase depends on access to real vehicle-tracking data.

  1. Now · prototype

    NH-44 on generated data

    • Delay model, quantile window, evaluator view
    • FastAPI backend and live corridor dashboard
    • Data-quality audit and hashed artifacts

  2. Next · real feeds

    AIS-140 GPS ingestion

    • Read live pings from AIS-140 vehicle tracking units
    • Re-train and re-test on real trips
    • Re-measure window coverage on live trips

  3. Then · pilot

    More Punjab corridors

    • Multi-route, multi-bus model
    • Low-bandwidth rider view and stop displays
    • Operator dashboard for depots

  4. Later · scale

    PM-eBus Sewa fleets

    • Bring the same window to new electric-bus fleets
    • Charging and dwell signals as features
    • Open API for journey planners
